# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project. Be respectful,
assume good intent, disagree with ideas rather than people, accept correction,
and make space for contributors with different experience levels and
backgrounds.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads,
and other spaces where a person represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
contact method listed on the GitHub organization or maintainer profile. Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made
without conflicts of interest. A maintainer involved in a report should recuse
themselves when another maintainer is available.
